# Hermes Philosophy

## Core Beliefs

### Knowledge is a Living Artifact
Knowledge is not static data. It is born from observation, validated through evidence, versioned through evolution, and refined through continuous cognitive cycles. It has lineage, confidence, and lifecycle.

### Understanding Precedes Action
No action without understanding. No proposal without problem. No execution without authorization. Every action traces to an observation through a complete chain of reasoning.

### Architecture is Constitution
The architecture is not implementation detail. It is the constitution. It defines what is permissible, what is forbidden, and why. The constitution is supreme. No emergency powers. No pragmatic exceptions.

### Separation of Concerns is Absolute
Each cognitive stage has exactly one responsibility. No stage may usurp another's constitutional role. Boundaries are enforced by architecture tests, not convention.

### Determinism is Non-Negotiable
Same inputs → same outputs. Always. Non-determinism is a bug, not a feature. Frozen dataclasses, deterministic algorithms, explicit seeds.

### Immutability is Default
All domain models are frozen. History is append-only. Supersession replaces mutation. No overwrites. No silent updates. History is immutable.

### Traceability is Sacred
Every decision traces to evidence. Every artifact traces to its origin. No decision without evidence. No artifact without lineage. The chain is unbroken.

### Uncertainty is Explicit
Confidence is quantified. Uncertainty is documented. "I don't know" is a valid, documented state. Certainty is never manufactured.

### Complexity is Managed, Not Hidden
Abstractions exist to clarify, not to hide. Every abstraction has a constitutional purpose. Leaky abstractions are constitutional violations.

### Evolution is Constitutional
Change happens through constitutional process. No ad-hoc modifications. No emergency patches. No pragmatic exceptions. The constitution governs its own amendment.

### Execution is the Terminal Act
Execution is the only layer that mutates state. It performs only what is authorized. It reports facts, not judgments. Its output becomes future observation.

## Anti-Patterns (Constitutional Violations)

| Anti-Pattern | Constitutional Violation |
|--------------|--------------------------|
| "Just this once" exception | No emergency powers |
| "Pragmatic" boundary crossing | Separation of concerns is absolute |
| "Just add a field" to domain | Domain is frozen; schema changes are constitutional amendments |
| "Optimize" by skipping stages | Pipeline order is constitutional |
| "Cache" domain objects | Domain objects are immutable values |
| "Smart" domain models | Domain models are pure data; behavior belongs to engines |
| "Quick fix" in domain | Domain is frozen; fixes are constitutional amendments |
| "Convenience" boundary crossing | Boundaries are constitutional, not conventional |

## The Hermetic Principle

> **What is not explicitly permitted by the Constitution is forbidden.**

The Constitution does not enumerate all forbidden actions. It enumerates permitted structures. Everything else is unconstitutional.

This is not restrictive. It is protective. It prevents the slow erosion of architectural integrity that kills every ambitious system.

## The Hermetic Oath

Every engine, every module, every line of code in Hermes swears:

> I will not cross my constitutional boundary.
> I will not mutate what I do not own.
> I will not reason where I should observe.
> I will not evaluate where I should propose.
> I will not govern where I should authorize.
> I will not execute where I should authorize.
> I will not mutate what I did not create.
> I will not invent evidence I did not observe.
> I will not invent permission I was not granted.
> I will not interpret what I should only report.
> I will trace every decision to its evidence.
> I will trace every artifact to its origin.
> I will supersede, never mutate.
> I will trace, never assume.
> I will freeze, never mutate.
> I will separate, never merge.
> I will verify, never assume.

This oath is not ceremonial. It is enforced by 420 architecture tests that run on every commit.
