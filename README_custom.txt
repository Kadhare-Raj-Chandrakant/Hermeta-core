# Hermes Brain

Hermes Brain is a **persistent intelligence operating system** designed to cultivate and preserve a user's evolving understanding, context, and knowledge *across all language model interactions and sessions*.

**This is not an AI agent, a chatbot, or a simple memory plugin.** Instead, Hermes Brain serves as the fundamental **intelligence layer** that sits *between you and any language model*, acting as your personal cognitive backbone, ensuring semantic continuity and knowledge accretion.

It transforms the fragmented, stateless nature of typical LLM engagements into a cohesive, intelligently evolving experience. By maintaining and enriching a dynamic understanding of your ongoing projects, interests, and past interactions, Hermes Brain allows language models to operate with a far deeper and more relevant context. This enables truly cumulative learning and eliminates the need for constant re-contextualization, empowering LLMs to act as true extensions of your intellectual process.
